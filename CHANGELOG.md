# Change log

## v0.4.0 · 2026-10-26

- User-approved Clear primary / Soft alternative, both Bold.
- Curved terminals on 24 Latin letters and eight digits compared with v0.3.1; other glyphs retain their previous designs.
- Shared short right tail on lowercase l; O/o/0/8 retained.
- Hangul (11,172 syllables), cmap and widths unchanged from v0.3.1.
- Outlines identical to the approved v0.4.0-preview.1 variants; final naming/version metadata and a Korean/English sample string added.
- Original copyright/license retained byte for byte. English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean documentation added.
